## Retention period

Every document below is kept at the disposal of the national competent authorities for
**10 years after the release it covers is placed on the market**. For release 1.0.0, placed on the
market on 2026-06-15, that is until at least **2036-06-15**. Each later release starts its own
10-year period. Documents are never deleted before the longest period that applies to them ends.

| Document (Article 18(1)) | Where it is kept | Retention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (a) Technical documentation: `technical-documentation.md` and the files it points to | This Git repository; yearly export to the document archive | 10 years after placing on the market of each release |
| (b) Quality management system documentation: `quality-management-system.md`, `risk-management.md`, `serious-incident-reporting.md`, this policy | This Git repository; yearly export to the document archive | 10 years after placing on the market of each release |
| (c) Changes approved by notified bodies | Not applicable: internal control, no notified body | |
| (d) Decisions and documents from notified bodies | Not applicable: internal control, no notified body | |
| (e) EU declaration of conformity: `eu-declaration-of-conformity.md` and the signed PDF | This Git repository; signed PDF in the document archive | 10 years after placing on the market of each release |

## How history is preserved

- The Git repository keeps every version of every document. `main` is protected: no force push,
  linear history, review before merge (`.github/settings.yml`).
- Every January, and at every release, the Quality and Regulatory Lead exports the documents above
  as signed PDFs to the provider's document archive, which has a 10-year retention lock.
- If Nekod Test Labs B.V. stops trading before a period ends, the Managing Director transfers the
  archive to a custodian who keeps it available to the authorities for the rest of the period.
